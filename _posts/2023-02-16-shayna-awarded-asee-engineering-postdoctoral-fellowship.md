Shayna Hilburg was selected as an eFellow in the [2022 cohort](https://efellows.asee.org/awardee) for her work on retrosynthesis and materials characterization of lipid nanoparticles for improved nucleic acid therapeutics.

The eFellows program is a two-year engineering postdoctoral fellowship administered by the American Society for Engineering Education (ASEE) with funding provided by the National Science Foundation (NSF). Find more information about the ASEE eFellows Program [here](https://efellows.asee.org/home).
