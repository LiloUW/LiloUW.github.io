Sage Scheiwiller successfully defended her dissertation, titled "Morphological Characterization of Conjugated Polymer Blends in Solid and Solution States Using Small Angle Scattering," on March 1st, 2024. Her presentation highlighted how understanding polymer blend morphology can allow for better property optimization. Sage now holds a PhD in Chemical Engineering.

Congrats, Dr. Scheiwiller! 🎉
