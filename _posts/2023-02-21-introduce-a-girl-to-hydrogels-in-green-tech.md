We were excited to participate in WChE's 7th annual Introduce a Girl event, which took place on February 18, 2023. This year's theme was green technology, and the Pozzo Group had fun educating young scientists about hydrogels and their many uses in the world around us.

The Introduce a Girl series aims to educate girls and gender expansive folks ages 5-15 about current research topics in STEM fields. More information about the event is available on the [WChE website](https://www.wcheuw.com/introduceagirl2023).
