Pozzo research group member Abdul Moeez is working at the Bundesanstalt für Materialforschung und -prüfung (BAM), the Federal Institute for Materials Research and Testing in Berlin, Germany, under the supervision of Dr. ir. Brian R. Pauw. The four-week trip encompasses full stack Small Angle X-Ray Scattering (SAXS) training, including instrumentation, experimentation and data analysis. His training also included hands-on experience with EPICS (Experimental Physics and Industrial Control System) and designing platforms that work with EPICS. This training will accelerate his own work on integrating microfluidic synthesis with SAXS to optimize the structure of nanoparticles. Outside work, he can be found exploring museums in Berlin, running with different running groups and trying cuisines from around the world.

Learn more about Moeez's experience [here](https://lookingatnothing.com/index.php/archives/4082).

His trip was supported by Clean Energy Institute (CEI) and Molecular Engineering Materials Center (MEMC) travel grants.
