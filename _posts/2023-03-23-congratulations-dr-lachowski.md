Kacper Lachowski successfully defended his dissertation, titled "Bottom-Up Synthesis of Colloidal Systems Using Sequence Defined Molecules," on March 10, 2023. His presentation highlighted his research contributions to the use of sequence defined molecules in the synthesis of colloidal materials. Kacper now holds a PhD in Molecular Engineering and Science.

Congrats, Dr. Lachowski!
