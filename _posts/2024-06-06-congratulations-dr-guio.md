Lorenzo Guio successfully defended his dissertation, titled "Understanding the Relationships between the Structure and the Physical Properties of Conjugated Polymers using Particle Scattering and Electronic Properties," on June 3rd, 2024. His presentation highlighted his work in furthering our understanding of novel conjugated polymers, and their potential for applications in semiconducting materials. Lorenzo now holds a PhD in Materials Science and Engineering.

Congratulations, Dr. Guio!
