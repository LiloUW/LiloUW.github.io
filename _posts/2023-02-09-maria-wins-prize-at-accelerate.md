Maria Politi was awarded a prize for her poster entitled "Open-Hardware Materials Acceleration Platforms for Accessible and Democratized Materials Discovery." She presented her work at ACCELERATE 2022, the first annual conference of the University of Toronto's [Acceleration Consortium](https://acceleration.utoronto.ca/). The conference explores cutting-edge advancements in artificial intelligence, automation, and data science for affordable, accelerated discovery of new materials.
