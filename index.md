The Pozzo Research Group in the [Department of Chemical Engineering](https://www.cheme.washington.edu/) at the University of Washington develops and controls self-assembly processes for soft materials at nano and micrometer scales.

We combine laboratory automation, high-throughput experimentation, and AI-driven approaches with small-angle X-ray and neutron scattering to accelerate materials discovery. Our work spans next-generation battery materials, functional emulsions for medical imaging, and open-source tools for autonomous experimentation.

Technologies developed in our laboratory have led to three startup companies: JanuTech, Membrion Inc., and PolyDrop LLC. Over 120 students have trained in the lab since 2007.
